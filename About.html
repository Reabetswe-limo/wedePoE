Our Story

"Founded in 2023 by Katleho Limo to bridge the gap between financial strain and living a healthy lifestyle."

PrimeFit was born from a simple, personal reality. Growing up in Bloemfontein, Katleho Limo was passionate about sport and physical development but routinely faced barriers due to the extreme costs associated with modern commercial gym subscriptions and dietary wellness supplements.

Upon establishing career stability, Katleho vowed to systematically dismantle those financial walls for the next generation. In 2023, PrimeFit opened its physical doors in Bloemfontein, instantly providing the community with structural budget access to elite lifting frameworks, clean locker rooms, and transparent advice.

Our Core Values

  • Inclusivity: A completely non-intimidating training environment designed warmly for beginners and competitive lifters alike.
  • Affordability: Strategic baseline pricing architectures optimized around student allowances.
  • Authenticity: Providing certified, pure sports nutrition lines to guarantee consumer protection.
